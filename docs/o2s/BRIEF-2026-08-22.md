One change today, and it is on the price that goes on the bag. It affects the
KAM when a PO is entered, the packer when a bag is filled, and QA when a truck
is inspected.

## The short version

Until yesterday the system asked one question about the price on the pack:
**does this PO print a price — yes or no?**

That question was wrong, and it was making your screens useless. Most of our bags
do not carry a price negotiated on a PO. They carry **VAN's standard list price**,
which is not a property of any order at all. Forced to answer yes or no, 136
product lines ended up reading *"MRP not recorded"* — which told the inspector
standing at the truck precisely nothing.

**There are now three answers.**

| The answer | What it means | What the bag should show |
|---|---|---|
| **Current list price** | The bag carries our standard MRP | Whatever is on the **latest price list** |
| **A price set on this PO** | A figure negotiated for that customer | That exact number, shown on your screen |
| **No price** | Nothing is printed | A bare bag. A price on it is a **failure** |

**Most of the range is the first one.** That is normal, and it is not a gap in
the record.

---

## If you are QA — one new box, and it is required

On a PO answered **"current list price"**, the inspection will not save until you
fill this in:

> **Price printed on the bag** \*
> The system does not hold the list price — it changes. Read the number off the
> bag, check it against the **latest price list**, and write it here.

It appears on the **pack inspection** and on the **pre-shipment (truck)
inspection**. Not on lot QA.

**Why you and not the system:** the price list is revised from time to time, and
old stock stays in the market at the old number. Eleven of our brands already
have two different prices on bags out there right now. A copy of the list kept
inside the system would go stale and would look authoritative while it was wrong
— which is more dangerous than holding no copy at all.

So **the paper price list is the authority, and you are the check.** Take the
current list with you.

**What you are actually confirming:** that the number printed on the bag matches
the current list. If it does not — that is a finding. Mark it and say so. Do not
quietly write down the number on the bag as though it were correct.

Over a few months these readings become the only record we have of which price
list revision each bag went out under. Today that record does not exist anywhere.

## If you are packing

Where a PO says **list price**, the screen now tells you to take the number from
the **latest price list** and record what you are printing. That is not an error
state and it is not a complaint — it is the normal case. Record what actually
goes on the bag.

Where a PO carries **its own price**, the number is shown to you. If what you are
printing is different, the screen will say so in red and the difference is
recorded. **It does not change the PO.** The PO's price is only ever set by the
KAM or the COO.

## If you are a KAM

New POs ask the three-way question and **will not submit until it is answered.**
There is no default. Whatever you pick, packing and QA are both shown it.

If you pick **"a price set on this PO"**, you must enter the number on every
line — otherwise QA is told a price is coming and given nothing to check it
against.

## Something you will see over the next few days

**POs that have not otherwise changed will start looking different.**

There is a backlog of orders created before this question existed. I am going
through and answering them. As I do, lines that read *"MRP not recorded"* in grey
will change to *"List price · check price list"*, or to a number, or to *"No price
on pack"*.

**That is me answering, not something going wrong.** It will happen over a few
days rather than all at once, and I will do it outside shift hours where I can.

Two other things you may notice, and neither is a fault:

- **Two products show "print price is not a real price"** — V-Borate 17% and
  V-NPK 20:20:20, both recorded at PKR 0.1 per pack. Somebody typed that to get
  past an old mandatory field. It is a record of an old workaround, not a new
  problem
- **A batch number carrying more than one manufacturing date** — around fourteen
  of these. They are historical. Those bags are printed and gone; the record
  cannot be rewritten to disagree with material that is already in the market

## What to do if a new item appears in your actions

You may see items you have not seen before. Some are genuinely new; some existed
as work but were never showing on anyone's screen.

**Please do this rather than working around it:**

1. **Do the item if you can.** If it is your job and it makes sense, just do it
2. **If you do not understand what it is asking — tell me the same day.** Do not
   guess, and do not clear it to make it go away. An item cleared without the
   work being done is worse than an item sitting there
3. **If it is not your job — tell me that too.** It means I have assigned it to
   the wrong role, and you are the only one who will notice
4. **If it will not clear even though you did the work — tell me.** That is a
   fault and I want it the same day, not at the end of the week

The same goes for anything that now blocks you where it did not before. **If a
control stops you doing your job and you believe it is wrong, say so.** I would
rather change it than have people find ways around it. Three people did exactly
that yesterday and all three things were fixed the same day.
