## Problem (existing — `product_concept`)

**V Germinator Pro — 100% nutrition, NPK + Fe + Mn.** Initial idea: a
starter/germination product carrying complete NPK plus trace Fe and Mn.

## What led here, in Himmayat's words

> During this period, we received a complaint from one of our vendors that the
> seals of Tornado bottles were leaking. Investigation showed that fermentation
> was occurring in the product. Since molasses is also present in V Germinator
> Pro, we considered fermentation as a potential stability risk for this product
> as well.
>
> Our initial hypothesis was that fermentation should not occur at such a low pH.
> However, when a sealed V Germinator Pro bottle was placed under observation for
> several days, the bottle seal was found to be swollen — confirming that
> fermentation could occur despite the low pH.
>
> The product is therefore currently under further stability observation to
> identify the source of fermentation. The main hypotheses being investigated are
> molasses and amino acids.

**One Claim already exists from this, and it stands as written:** *"Fermentation
should not occur at such a low pH"* — believed, tested (the sealed-bottle
observation), **disproved**. This pilot does not reopen that Claim; it asks the
next question the disproof left open.

## Bet 1 — molasses is the source

**Belief:** Molasses is the fermenting component in V Germinator Pro.

**Kill criterion — written before the next bench work, not after:**
Formulate a sample with molasses removed and everything else held constant
(including the amino acid component). Seal it and observe under the same
conditions as the original trial. If the seal does **not** swell / no
fermentation is observed over an equivalent observation period, molasses is
confirmed as the source and this Bet passes. If it swells anyway, this Bet is
killed.

## Bet 2 — amino acids are the source

**Belief:** The amino acid component is the fermenting component in V
Germinator Pro.

**Kill criterion — written before the next bench work, not after:**
Formulate a sample with the amino acid component removed and molasses held in,
everything else constant. Seal it and observe under the same conditions. If the
seal does **not** swell, amino acids are confirmed as the source and this Bet
passes. If it swells anyway, this Bet is killed.

_These two Bets are deliberately run as a pair, not a sequence — the point is to
isolate which one variable is doing it, not to guess and retry serially._
