# HG26026 — the 2,800 Kg "already packed" report — 26 August 2026

**Reported by the COO, screenshots attached:** PO 0254 (KISAN FERTILISER PVT LTD),
line "Germinator Pro - Humic," reads Produced 2,800/3,200 and Packed 2,800/3,200.
Production said they had not packed it.

## What the live system actually holds (read-only check, nothing changed)

Checked directly against the live app's in-memory state (no database access, no
edits made):

- **`state.packingLog`** carries one real entry against this PO line: id
  `PK2074-1vih` — 2,800 Kg of Potassium Humate, packed from base batch **HG26026**
  into brand batch **GPH26002**, against **PO 0254**, dated **2026-08-22**,
  recorded by **Ali Raza**, price-verified by Ali Raza the same moment
  (07:09:26 UTC). Not yet shipped (`shipKg: 0`) — it is sitting in finished-goods
  stock, packed but not dispatched.
- **`state.actionLog`** has the matching line: *"Packed 2,800 Kg/L Potassium
  Humate [HG26026] → Germinator Pro - Humic [GPH26002] for 0254,"* role
  **Production**, by **Ali Raza**, same timestamp.
- The batch's own numbers agree with the PO line: `HG26026.packedKg = 2,800`.
- The timeline is orderly and internally consistent: lot HG26026-L1 (2,800 Kg,
  Morning shift, Muhammad Sajjad) produced **2026-08-20** → its certificate
  approved **2026-08-21** → packed **2026-08-22**. HG26026 has since grown to
  8 lots / 39,400 Kg produced, all certified; only that first 2,800 Kg has been
  packed so far.

**Conclusion: this is not a display bug and not a phantom number.** There is a
real, named, dated pack transaction behind the 2,800 Kg, and every figure that
depends on it (PO line, batch, action log, packing log) agrees with every other
one. Nothing found suggests the system fabricated or double-counted this.

## What is NOT known from inside the app

The app has no screen that shows this history to anyone — `actionLog` and
`packingLog` are written on every action but never rendered anywhere. Nobody
on the floor has a way to check "was this really packed" without someone
reading the raw data the way this check just did. That is a real gap, separate
from this incident, and worth fixing later (a simple batch/PO "history" tab).

So the open question is not "did the system get this wrong" — it did not — but
**why the production team said nothing was packed** when Ali Raza's own account
recorded packing it four days ago. Possibilities, not established either way:
he packed it and whoever the COO spoke to did not know; someone is not
remembering correctly; or (less likely, given how complete and specific the
record is — a real brand-batch number, correct mfg/exp dates, a price
verification step) the entry was made without the physical pack happening.

**Recommended next step:** ask Ali Raza directly whether he packed 2,800 Kg of
Potassium Humate into batch GPH26002 against PO 0254 on 22 August. Nothing in
the record should be changed on the strength of a guess — if he confirms it
did not happen, that is a correction (`REVERSE` on the pack) done deliberately
with a reason, not a silent edit.
