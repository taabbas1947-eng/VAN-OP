## Your first day in 5 steps
Yawar:
1. Open **Approvals**. Do "Your next move", then the rest of your list.
2. Open **Products**. Each client is a row: open it for its products, then a product for its recipe, cost by pack and history.
3. Open **Client prices**. Pick a client, type a new price or target in the row, see the GP at once, then Send to Tahir.
4. Open **Margin**. It opens on the whole company for this month; open a line, then a pack, down to each truck.
5. Open **Setup, Costs** when a material, packing, labour or FOH rate changes. Every change is dated and logged.

Tahir:
1. Open **Approvals**. Do "Your next move", then the prices, list revisions, targets and settings waiting for you.
2. Open **Client prices**. A price from Yawar shows "Open to approve" in its row.
3. Open **Margin**. It opens on the whole company for this month; open a line, then a pack, down to each truck.
4. Open **Products**. Each client is a row: open it for its products, then a product for its recipe, cost by pack and history.
5. Open **Setup, Settings** for KAM lists and Roles.

"Who does what in the recipe chain" is folded under the table; "Your look" (the colour scheme, kept per browser) is under it.

## Everyone else: your job in C2M
A role made in Setup, Settings, Roles reads: "This role was set up by the administrator in Setup, Settings, Roles. What you may do is what is ticked on it: the places you see are the places you may use." (and the lines after it in GUIDE_CUSTOM, c2m.html).

### Majid, Production Manager (role production)
1. Today shows recipes to check or revise; each opens in a panel.
2. Write a new version from Recipes when a recipe changes, including a one-time change.
3. A recipe sent back carries a reason and advice; revise and resend it, and it goes straight back to whoever sent it.
4. Products: every client product, to read: its recipe, packs and cost. Nothing on it changes a price or a cost.
5. Add a variant: Products, + New, Add a variant, or the product's page, Add a variant. Name the variant and its packs; its recipe starts as a copy of the product's, as a draft. Change what differs and send it on like any recipe. Labour and overhead for a variant are set by Yawar.

### Himayat, QC Head (role qc)
1. Today shows recipes to review and materials to complete; each opens in a panel.
2. Inputs: class each material (nutrient source, or no nutrients such as binder, filler, anti-caking agent, wetting agent) and enter the % of each nutrient field.
3. Check the inputs and the nutrient analysis, then mark reviewed or send back with a reason and advice.

### Fahim, Plant Manager (role plant)
1. Today shows recipes to endorse; each opens in a panel.
2. Check the recipe, nutrients and cost, then endorse or send back with a reason and advice.

### KAMs and managers (role viewer)
1. Price lists shows the lists granted to you.
2. Download the PDF to share. Prices are approved prices only.
3. If no list shows, none is granted to you yet: ask Tahir or Yawar.

### How C2M works (shown to everyone else)
1. Each client product has its own recipe: Majid writes, Himayat reviews, Fahim endorses, Yawar approves cost.
2. Yawar keeps rates current; C2M recosts every recipe.
3. Yawar proposes prices; Tahir approves; managers see the approved lists.
4. A product can have variants (Humi Grow: Powder, Pellets); a variant's recipe starts as a copy.
5. KAMs see only the lists and clients Yawar or Tahir give them. Cost and margin never reach them.
6. Every change is dated and logged.

## The 4 product words
- **Product**: what VAN makes and sells under its own brand (Humi Grow).
- **Variant**: a form of 1 product (Humi Grow Powder, Humi Grow Pellets). Each variant has its own recipe, packs, cost and rates.
- **Pack**: 1 size of a product or client product (1 kg, 25 kg), with its own packing bill, cost and price.
- **Client product**: a product as one client buys it, under the client's brand name. It has its own recipe, or follows one of our products' recipe.
