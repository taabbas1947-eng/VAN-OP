O2S Access Matrix

Who can do what inside Order‑to‑Ship. Every action clears three gates — the right role, ownership of the item, and how the right is enforced. The COO sits above all of it; the in‑app Authorisation panel is the live word.

GATE 1

Role

You hold an O2S role that carries the right.

GATE 2

Ownership

You act on your own item — unless it was formally escalated to you. Holding the role is not enough.

GATE 3

Enforcement

How the check is made: panel‑configurable, hard‑coded, open, COO‑only, or a permanent sign‑off.

Enforcement — tap to filter
● owns the right ○ reachable via escalation ◆ COO — admin, all rights

The ownership rule

A role is necessary but not sufficient — O2S checks the item's owner too. A manager only sees another owner's live button when work is formally escalated to them. This is the 2026‑07‑30 rule: one person acting on another's work must stay impossible.

COO‑only holds

Customer create / amend are held to the COO (not the KAM) until customer rows carry ids — two people on that screen at once silently delete each other's work. The hold comes off when the ids land.

Second signatures

Releasing a truck, approving a delivery challan, reopening a batch, and rework after an UNFIT lab result are the Plant Manager's sign‑offs — a second person checking a first's work. They stay hard‑coded and are never delegated.

Panel vs code

Rights move into the Authorisation panel one department at a time. Commercial, Supply Chain, Production are converted (Aug 2026); Quality is still enforced in code. A right only appears in the panel once a real button asks for it.
