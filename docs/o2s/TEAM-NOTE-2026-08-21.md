Three things changed on your screens this week. All three came from watching
where the system was making your job harder instead of easier.

## 1 · You can now see the price that goes on the pack

Until now, the price to be printed on the bag was only visible to the person
doing the packing. Nobody else — not the KAM who set it, not Supply Chain
loading the truck, not QA inspecting before dispatch — could see it anywhere.

That is why price mistakes kept getting through. It was not carelessness. The
number simply was not on anyone's screen.

It is now, in five places: the PO Tracker, the Load-a-truck screen, and all
three inspection screens. You will see one of four things:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **MRP 1,250 /pack** (amber) | This is the price that must be on the bag. Check it |
| **No price on pack** (grey) | This client does not want a price printed. A bag with a price on it is **wrong** |
| **MRP not set** (red) | This PO is supposed to print a price and nobody has entered one. Ask the KAM |
| **Print price not specified** (grey) | An older PO from before we started recording this. Check the customer PO |

**Not every order carries a printed price, and that is fine.** Grey is not a
problem. Red is.

## 2 · The boxes are big enough to write in

Remarks, notes, delay reasons and correction notes were single-line boxes that
showed about twenty characters. You could not read what you had typed. So people
wrote "late" or "issue" and moved on, and we ended up with a system full of
records that explain nothing.

Those are now proper boxes, three lines tall, and you can drag them bigger.

**Please use them.** When something goes wrong, the sentence you write is the
only thing that will still be there in six months. "Truck held at Sahiwal, driver
changed, 4 hours" is worth a hundred times more than "delay".

## 3 · Screens fit the screen

Forms were locked to a narrow width no matter how big your monitor. Dropdowns
were cutting off the product name so you could not read what you had selected.
Date boxes were clipping. All fixed.

If anything still looks squeezed on your machine, tell me which browser you use.

---

## What I am asking for

**Enter it when it happens, not at the end of the week.**

I know some of you are catching up in batches. I understand why — the system was
hard to use, so it went to the bottom of the list. That part is now my problem
to fix, and I am fixing it.

But it has to work both ways. A pre-shipment inspection recorded five days after
the truck left is not an inspection. It is paperwork. The whole reason we built
this was so that nothing leaves the factory unless somebody actually checked it —
the quality, the packing, the price, the batch number, the manufacturing and
expiry dates. That only means something if the check happens before the gate,
and the record happens the same day.

## What is coming next

I am changing the system so it records **two dates** on every entry: the date the
thing actually happened, and the date it was keyed in. Right now those are the
same field, which means a late entry is invisible and the system cannot tell me
anything useful.

I am telling you this before I build it, not after. It is not there to catch
anyone out. It is there because I cannot fix what I cannot see, and right now I
am flying blind on where work is really getting stuck.

If there is a reason you cannot record something on the day — the terminal is
down, you are on the floor and away from a screen, the paperwork reaches you
late — **tell me now.** I would rather change the system to fit how the work
actually happens than add a rule that makes everyone lie to it.

— Tahir

# Addendum — end of the same day

*Draft for Tahir. The note above went out mid-morning; a good deal changed after

---

Since this morning, three of you raised things and all three are fixed.

**Majid** asked for a formal way to correct data-entry mistakes. You are right,
and it is the next thing being built. Right now some records genuinely cannot be
corrected at all — a packing run, a passed inspection, an approved COA. That is
not acceptable in a system people are learning on, and it is being fixed properly
rather than patched.

**Fahim** reported that gate pass approval never appeared in his actions. It was
worse than that: **the entire truck process after "Ship" was missing from
everyone's worklist** — start loading, issue gate pass, release, approve DC,
confirm delivery. Not one of them raised an item. So when I said people were not
responding to the Action Center, for half the shipment process **there was
nothing there to respond to.** That is on the system, not on you. All five now
appear.

**Fahim** also asked for a mobile version. The app was pushing the page sideways
on a phone on seven screens out of twelve, and you had to scroll most of a screen
before seeing a single task. Both fixed.

## What is different on your screens

**The price on the pack is now everyone's business.** It used to be visible only
to the person packing. It now shows on the PO Tracker, on the truck screen and on
all three inspection screens, in one of four states — a price, *no price on
pack*, *not set*, or *not recorded*. **Not every PO prints a price and that is
perfectly normal** — grey is not a problem, red is.

**New POs must answer one question:** *does this PO print a price on the pack?*
There is no default and the PO will not submit until it is answered. Packing and
QA are both shown your answer, so it has to be an answer rather than a blank.

**Packing no longer sets the PO's price.** Whatever gets typed at packing is
recorded as *what went on the bag*; the PO's authorised price is only ever set by
the KAM or the COO. If the two differ, the inspector sees it flagged. Also — a PO
set to print **no** price can now be packed at all, which it could not before.

**The inspection has changed the most, and there is a new SOP (v2.0).** Three
record checks with the expected values shown, a proper sample size from the
standard, and the true date of the inspection rather than the date you typed it.
**Please read the SOP before your next inspection** — v1.0 from this morning is
withdrawn and one of its instructions is now wrong.

**A truck can no longer be released without a passing pre-shipment inspection.**
The Plant Manager's release button refuses. QA is a gate now, not paperwork.

**Every entry records two dates** — when it happened and when it was keyed. Enter
the real date. If it is more than a day or two old you will be asked why, and a
plain sentence is all that is wanted.

## Two things you will notice, and they are not faults

**Overdue and escalation numbers will get worse.** They now count from real dates
instead of dates that were themselves entered late. Amber where it used to be
green is the system telling the truth for the first time.

**Certificates will say "no individual name on file"** where a name should be.
That is because our logins are named after jobs, not people. It is being fixed at
my end. Nothing you need to do.

## What I am asking, again

Enter it on the day. Not at the end of the week.

I know the system made that hard, and a lot of what changed today was making it
less hard — bigger boxes you can actually read, the missing items now showing in
your actions, a phone that works. If something still makes same-day recording
harder than it should be, **tell me and it gets fixed.** That has now happened
three times today and each one was real.

— Tahir
