# AP26012 — the certified duplicate, and the way back that did not exist
**26 August 2026, afternoon · independent review: four reviewers, first round refused · see "What the reviewers found"**

## What happened after the morning's push

The morning's build (`AP26012-2026-08-26.md`) put a Remove control on each lot and,
on your decision, made it refuse any lot the lab has touched. Then the lab certified
the duplicate lot **AP26012-L2** by mistake. The refusal was right. The way back was
not there.

- The only correction a signed certificate takes is **Supersede** (SPEC-03: no Amend,
  no Reverse — a printed copy may be outside the building).
- The only button that starts one lives on the **certificate sheet**.
- **No screen ever opened that sheet for an approved certificate.** Lab QC → Approved
  offered Print COA and nothing else — in every version back to 21 August. The
  baselines in `o2s/tests/` are rendered to prove it (`certremove.test.js` §1).

So you stood in front of a refusal with no route out, and the batch offered
**Pack 2,020 Kg cleared** on 1,010 Kg of real material.

I first told you the Supersede route worked. I had checked it by calling the
function, not by rendering the screen — the exact trap `o2s/tests/README.md` warns
about. I then rendered every screen and tab as COO and QCM, found no way to click
into it, and withdrew the advice the same hour.

## What is built (all rendered and clicked in the suite, not searched for)

1. **Open** beside Print COA on Lab QC → Approved. Opens the existing read-only
   certificate sheet; Supersede inside it is still QCM / COO only.
2. **The reason is written where you stand.** On the batch passport → Lots, the
   holder of the Remove right (Plant Manager, COO) now sees a greyed Remove on a
   refused lot with the reason under it — including the route: *Lab QC → Approved →
   Open → Supersede*. Before, a refused lot simply had no button and the reason lived
   in a toast that only a race could trigger. Four reviewers found this independently.
3. **A re-issued draft says so, everywhere the lab meets it.** After a supersede the
   draft shows "Revision 1 — a re-issue … superseded by … : reason" as the first thing
   on the sheet, a note on the Lab QC row, and the reason in the Lab Rep's My Actions
   label. Without this the lab was handed the phantom lot as ordinary work, pre-filled,
   and three clicks would have re-certified it.
4. **The certified floor.** Removing a lot may not take produced below what the lab
   has certified on the batch's other lots — the same floor the produced-quantity
   correction has. This exists for one reason, below.
5. **Lots vs produced, stated when they differ** on the Lots tab. On the 16 July
   snapshot no batch trips it; it is a statement of two figures, not an alarm.
6. The Remove dialogue names any superseded certificate the lot carries and says its
   printable copy leaves with the lot; the register line names the certificate —
   number, revision, who approved it and when, who superseded it and why.
7. A draft lot reads **Draft** on the passport, not "In review".
8. The in-chain refusal says **Reject** (the button's real name) instead of "withdraw".

## 16:20 — AP26012 is a MULTI-PO batch

One step into the repair the COO sent the screen: L2 read *Draft*, Remove greyed,
"This batch serves several POs by allocation, so one shift's output cannot be
un-logged without disturbing them." AP26012 is a multi-PO batch — one run feeding
several POs — not the bulk batch the morning's note and every fixture assumed. The
morning's Remove refused every multi batch flat.

**Built (afternoon, second build):** on a multi batch, Remove takes the exact shares
of that shift back off each linked PO line, using the production-log rows the shift
wrote — one row per PO with the exact figure — rather than recomputing them (a second
submission can be capped differently from the first when a line is already full).
Those rows are removed; every line touched is listed in the register cascade and the
toast. The rows of one submission are found by allocation order, and the right
submission's rows by the lot's rank among same-signature lots; if the rows are not on
file the removal is still refused, with a message that says so. A test built the
duplicate through the real `submitShiftLog` twice and caught the first version
reversing the older submission's rows (the rank was computed after the lot had
already left the batch); pinned in `certremove.test.js` §12.

## Repairing AP26012 — the exact clicks, and the one condition that matters

**The condition.** A removal in this app is undone by any other O2S tab that loaded the
batch before the removal and later saves *anything* — a KAM typing a note on a
different PO is enough. It is not about anyone touching AP26012. So: do this when
nobody else is in O2S (end of day), or tell everyone to close O2S first, and tell
everyone to reload O2S before they save anything once you are done. A tab someone
left open and hidden brings L2 back hours later — as certified again. Measured by
the reviewers with the real merge; not a theory.

Also: **tell Production not to pack from AP26012 until it reads 1,010.** Packing
2,020 first makes the repair impossible without a packing reversal.

1. **Lab QC → Approved** (Group: by product helps — the tab has 120+ rows and no
   search) → **AP26012-L2** → **Open**.
2. **Supersede — re-issue**. Reason *Duplicate entry*; say the certificate was issued
   against a lot that was never made. **Supersede — issue a revision.**
3. **Reload the page.** Then **Production → Running batches → AP26012 → Lots.** L2 must
   read *Draft* with a live **Remove**; L1 stays greyed (certified — correct).
4. **Remove** on L2. Reason *Duplicate entry*, one sentence. **Remove this output.**
   Wait for the "Removed —" message, count five, then reload.
5. Expect **one lot · produced 1,010 · Pack 1,010 Kg cleared.** Now tell everyone to
   reload O2S, then press **Pack 1,010 Kg cleared**.

If step 3 shows L2 greyed with "already packed": someone packed first — stop, tell me;
the packing has to be reversed before the lot can go.

**If you later see two lots again** (an amber note says the lots total 2,020 but
produced reads 1,010): another tab put L2 back. Do not press Remove again (the app
refuses), do not correct produced upward, do not log a shift to make the figures match.
Tell me. Recovery exists for both shapes it can take — L2 back as Draft, or L2 back
as FIT with the supersede erased — but it means everyone out of O2S first.

If instead you see **two lots, L2 Draft, produced 2,020**: the removal never reached
the server (a save that failed silently). Press Remove again.

## What the reviewers found — the first round refused, and was right

All four (code, design, workflow, data-safety) refused the first build for the same
reason: the message this change existed to show was never displayed. That is fixed
(item 2). They also found:

**Open — needs a build of its own: a removal does not survive the team.** The app's
3-way merge cannot express a deleted row. A lot removed in one tab comes back the
moment any other open tab's copy is merged — while produced keeps the lowered
figure. Measured with the real merge in `certremove.test.js` §9: after the merge,
**two lots of 1,010 and produced 1,010**. A second Remove would then take produced
to **0** under a certified 1,010. Today's build stops the second Remove (the
certified floor) and states the mismatch on the Lots tab; it does not stop the
first resurrection. Reversals, voids and supersedes in this app are flags, not
splices, for this exact reason (a few older paths — `_voidReverseBatch`, the
certificate's own rework — do splice or drop, and are on the same list). **Remove must become a strike-through** (`removed: {at, by,
reason}` on the lot, the shift entry and the log row, every reader skipping them).
That also keeps Rev 0 on file, which settles the next item. Needs its own review.

**Decision recorded: Rev 0 leaves with the lot.** You chose "the register line is
enough" over keeping the superseded certificate printable. Note what that means: the
signed certificate's test results, analyst / reviewer / approver signatures and dates
exist nowhere afterwards; the register keeps number, revision, approver, date,
superseder and reason. SPEC-03 says nothing is ever deleted, and the SUPERSEDE
register entry says "Rev 0 … still prints". The strike-through above would make both
true again. Until then this is your recorded exception (noted in SPEC-03).

**Open — a two-signature route around disposal.** A lot that WAS made can now be
superseded (QCM / COO) and removed (Plant Manager / COO): produced drops, packed and
disposed do not move, the disposal register stays empty. The correction register
shows both acts, cross-referenced and reasoned — an honest trail, but one a stock
count will not see. The COO alone can do both. Your call whether that is acceptable.

**Open — the UNFIT rule is hollow today (live before this session).** Plant Manager:
UNFIT → *Send to rework* (no reason, no register entry, the certificate is simply
dropped) → the lot reads Pending → Remove. The same person holds both. The rule you
confirmed this morning — failed material needs a decision, not a deletion — needs
rework to leave a trace that Remove respects. Not built: it means a stored marker on
the lot, which is a data-model change I would not make without your say-so.

**Open — smaller.** The Supersede dialogue quotes the batch's packed and shipped
figures as if they were this lot's. *Reject* takes an empty reason and writes no
register entry. Superseded revisions do not appear in Reports → QC register. A
failed save is silent (`saveNow` swallows the error) — pre-existing. The register's
"Changed" column does not wrap, so the new certificate line makes a wide row.
