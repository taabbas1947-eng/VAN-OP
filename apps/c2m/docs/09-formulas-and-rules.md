A pack takes 1 labour rate and 1 FOH rate, each the first match of these levels: (1) the client's pack (a rate listing the product, with its pack range and form); (2) the variant of the product; (3) its product type; (4) a rate for all products, then the base bands. A pack's size has at most 3 decimals; a liquid is sold per L, a solid per kg.
