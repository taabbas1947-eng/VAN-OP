The DC point

The Delivery Challan is raised at shipment.plan — "Plan a shipment / raise a DC" — by Supply Chain. The Plant Manager then approves the DC and releases the truck as a second signature.

Branch 1 — materials

If RM Check comes back partial or none, a Purchase Requisition is raised and waits on CFO approval before material is received and the order can move to RM Ready.

Branch 2 — lab result

An UNFIT lab result doesn't kill the order — the Plant Manager accepts a deviation or sends the pool back for rework. Only a FIT result lets Production pack the cleared stock.

Stale delivery

Delivery confirmation stays with Supply Chain permanently — it never hands to the Plant Manager. If a confirmation goes stale it re‑flags to the same owner after 3 days.
